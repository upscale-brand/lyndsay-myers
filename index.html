Her approach

Food connects us. Nutrition guidance can honor that connection while helping you move toward your health goals.

All foods fit in a life shaped around you.

Lyndsay believes people can enjoy the foods they love while working toward nutrition goals. Her aim is to help clients leave appointments with a clearer understanding of food choices in different situations.

Her story

A curiosity about food became a calling.

Lyndsay’s path brings together nutrition science, personal connection, and a passion for diabetes care.

Curiosity

A change of direction

Lyndsay began college planning to become a pharmacist. An introductory nutrition class sparked a new interest, and she changed her major to nutrition.

Connection

Food brings us together

She values both the science of nutrition and the ways food connects people and families.

Purpose

A focus on diabetes

Family members living with Type 1 diabetes inspired Lyndsay’s interest in diabetes nutrition and education.
